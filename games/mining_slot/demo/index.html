How to play

Match 3 or more of a paying symbol across consecutive reels from the left. This prototype displays the 6×5 layout and feature flow.

  • Expanding Wild (W): a Wild expands to fill its reel and remains sticky during the free-spin feature.
  • Scatter (S): trigger-only; 3 Scatters start Gold Rush (10 spins), 4 start Crystal Cavern (12 spins), and 5–6 start Deep Mine Jackpot (15 spins).
  • Gold Rush: starts at 1×, no multiplier increase per spin.
  • Crystal Cavern: starts at 1× and increases by 0.25× after each spin.
  • Deep Mine Jackpot: starts at 1× and increases by 0.50× after each spin.
  • Retriggers are enabled. Feature spin budget is capped at 30 and multiplier at 5×. Round win cap: 15,000× initiating bet.

Paytable: Miner, Cart, Gold, Purple, Blue, Red, Green, Pickaxe, Lantern, A, K, Q, J, 10, 9. Exact payouts are part of the SDK math configuration, not this visual demo.
